# Research Foundation

HRIDAY builds on several complementary research directions rather than claiming that one paper solves the complete problem.

## 1. Mani et al. — CVPRW 2020

Automatic digitization of engineering diagrams using deep learning and graph search. Relevant to symbol detection, text association and connection discovery.

## 2. Paliwal et al. — PAKDD 2021

Digitize-PID. Relevant to P&ID-specific extraction and domain-guided validation/correction.

## 3. Bai et al. — CVPR 2026

End-to-end hyper-relational information extraction for engineering diagrams. Relevant to structured relational graph extraction.

## 4. Zhu et al. — 2026

From P&ID Drawings to Process Graphs: a multimodal approach. Relevant to separating visual extraction from explicit topology reconstruction.

## 5. Alimin & Schweidtmann — AIChE Journal 2026

GraphRAG for engineering diagrams / ChatP&ID. Relevant to graph-based natural-language interaction with P&IDs.

HRIDAY's engineering direction is to integrate these ideas into a sovereign workflow with evidence-linked graph facts and human verification for uncertain interpretations.
